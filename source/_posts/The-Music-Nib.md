# The Music Nib 

The pen is the William Mitchell No. 0268 Music Writing Nib.

# The add-ons

I purchased the under Slip-On Reservoir from the same William Mitchell company and some very good Sepia and Cyan ink bottles from an old company in Germany.

# Results

My coursive writing improved by an order of magnitude. I'm planning to write some Christmas letters for my loved ones and to gift them in December. 

I even purchased some red wax and a snowflake stamp to press the wax when I'll seal the letter.
<img src="stamp.jpg" alt="Sepia Ink" style="display: block; margin-left: 0; margin-right: auto; width: 600px; max-width: 100%;">

"Writing cursive is an act of resistance !" 

# Looking back

My writing with a normal ball pen is incomprehensible. I really believe now they should be used only in space..they make you haste the writing and give the writer some sort of anxiety - wanting to finish the act of writing as fast as possible.

# Advice
Everybody should try an old-school pen, at least once..I am sure most people won't be dissapointed and even they'll appreciate more the act of writing.
